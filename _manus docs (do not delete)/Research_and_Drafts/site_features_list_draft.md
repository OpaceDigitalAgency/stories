# StoriesFromTheWeb.org: Comprehensive Features List (c. 2000-2020)

This document lists the features observed on StoriesFromTheWeb.org across various versions archived between 2000 and 2020, based on Wayback Machine snapshots and third-party descriptions.

## Early Phase (c. 2000-2005)

**Content Consumption:**
*   **Read Stories:** Access to extracts from books by featured contemporary children's authors.
*   **Read Poems:** Dedicated section for poetry.
*   **Author Information:** Details about featured authors.
*   **Book Features:** Spotlights on specific popular books (e.g., Harry Potter).

**User Interaction & Creation:**
*   **Email Authors:** Facility for users to send emails to featured authors (likely moderated).
*   **Write Reviews:** Submission forms for users to write and send in book reviews.
*   **Write Your Own Stories:** Submission forms for users to send in their original stories.
*   **Submit Poetry:** Facility for users to submit their own poems.
*   **Competitions:** Regular competitions for users to enter.

**Community & Display:**
*   **Gallery:** A section showcasing user-submitted content, primarily book reviews initially, potentially expanding to stories and poems later.
*   **Library Clubs:** Information about associated physical computer clubs held in partner libraries (Birmingham, Bristol, Leeds), including how to join and which libraries were involved.

**Site Structure & Information:**
*   **Main Navigation Sections:** Typically included "Stories" (content), "Dig In" (interactive elements), "Library Clubs", and "Gallery".
*   **"Boring Adult Info":** Section containing project details, contact information (UKOLN email initially), funding acknowledgements (Library and Information Commission), and partner lists (UKOLN, Birmingham, Bristol, Leeds Libraries).
*   **Age Segmentation (by 2005):** Introduction of separate site portals/experiences tailored for different age groups (e.g., 8-11 and 11-14).

## Membership Phase (c. 2010-2020)

**Access Model:**
*   **Members-Only Access:** The entire site transitioned to a subscription-based model.
*   **Login System:** Required username and password, typically provided via a subscribing library authority.
*   **Subscription Information:** Details for libraries, schools, and potentially parents/carers on how to subscribe or gain access.
*   **Site Demo:** A publicly accessible demonstration of the site's features, likely aimed at prospective institutional subscribers.

**Content & Features (within Member Area - inferred from navigation/labels):**
*   **Writing Gallery:** Showcase for user-submitted written work.
*   **Art Gallery:** Showcase for user-submitted artwork.
*   **SFTW Blog:** Updates, news, writing prompts, or other site-related content.
*   **Book Awards:** Information related to children's literature awards.
*   **Interactive Sections (Labels observed):** "Inspiration" (likely writing prompts or ideas), "Watch" (potentially videos or animations), "Forum" (user discussion area), "Creative Help" (writing tips or resources), "Books and Authors" (expanded content on literature).
*   **Rating System ("Rate It!"):** Functionality for users to rate books or content.
*   **Express Yourself:** General area encouraging user creativity and submissions.

**Technology & Design:**
*   **Flash-Based Interface:** Significant portions of the site, particularly navigation and interactive elements, relied on Adobe Flash during this period.
*   **Updated Branding:** Refreshed logos and visual design compared to the early phase.

**Partnerships:**
*   **Publisher Logos:** Display of logos from various publishing partners (e.g., Corgi, Doubleday, Egmont, Hutchinson, Macmillan, O'Brien Press), suggesting content or promotional agreements.
*   **Library of Birmingham:** Prominently featured as a key partner/manager.

**Note:** The exact functionality of features within the members-only area (post-2010) is inferred from labels and surrounding context, as direct access was not possible via Wayback Machine without a valid login.
