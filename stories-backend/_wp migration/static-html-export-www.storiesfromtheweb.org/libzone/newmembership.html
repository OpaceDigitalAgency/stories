Stories from the Web
is a reader development website
managed by Digital Ink Drop CIC

Membership is available on a subscription basis
to Library Authorities within the UK.

You will have full use of the website for all children, young people & their families,
living within the area, who are members of their local library.
The site may then be used from their homes as well as in the library.

Library staff have access to the site and to the extra resources in the staff area.

If you are thinking of subscribing, or would like a trial period, then please contact us at:
info@storiesfromtheweb.org
We will inform you of the cost of subscription and answer any questions you may have.
