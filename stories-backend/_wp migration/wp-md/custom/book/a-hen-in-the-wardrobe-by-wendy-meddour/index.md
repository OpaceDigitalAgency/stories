## A Hen in the Wardrobe Plot

Follow Ramzi's journey as he travels with his parents to Algeria to meet his Berber relatives and discover the importance of family and the beauty of his Algerian heritage. Along the way, Ramzi's dad starts acting strangely and sleepwalking, searching for a hen. Join Ramzi on a funny and heartwarming adventure that celebrates family and cultural identity.
