## Earwig and the Witch Plot

Meet Earwig, a young orphan who is content with her life at St. Morwald's Home for Children until she is adopted by a witch named Bella Yaga. Earwig must use her wit and cleverness to outsmart the witch and navigate the magical world she finds herself in. This enchanting story is filled with humour, talking cats, and unexpected twists.
