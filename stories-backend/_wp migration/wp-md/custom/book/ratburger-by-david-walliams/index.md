## Ratburger Plot

Meet Zoe, a young girl who finds solace in her pet rat, Armitage. When Armitage goes missing and Zoe discovers a sinister burger van owner with a dark secret, she embarks on a daring mission to save her beloved rat. This hilarious and heartwarming story explores themes of friendship, family, and standing up against bullies.
