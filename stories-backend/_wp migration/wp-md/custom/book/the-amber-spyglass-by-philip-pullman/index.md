## Book & Author Info

**Genre:** Children's Fiction, Fantasy

**Series:** His Dark Materials

**Book Age Range:** 12+

## The Amber Spyglass Plot

Follow Lyra and Will as their worlds collide and they embark on a perilous journey to save all they hold dear. In the epic conclusion of the His Dark Materials trilogy, Lyra and Will face incredible challenges, encounter fascinating characters, and explore the mysteries of parallel universes. This captivating and thought-provoking tale explores love, destiny, and the power of human connection.
