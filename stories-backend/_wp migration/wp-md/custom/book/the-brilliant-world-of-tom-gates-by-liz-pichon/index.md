## The Brilliant World of Tom Gates Plot

Tom Gates is a master of excuses and doodling. Follow his hilarious and relatable diary as he navigates school, family, and his band, DogZombies. Packed with doodles, funny illustrations, and Tom's quirky observations, this book will keep you entertained from beginning to end.
