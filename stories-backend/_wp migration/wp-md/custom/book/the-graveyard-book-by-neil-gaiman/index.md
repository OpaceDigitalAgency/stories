## Book & Author Info

**Book Title:** The Graveyard Book

**Book Author:** Neil Gaiman

**Genre:** Children's Fiction, Fantasy

**Book Age Range:** 9-12

## The Graveyard Book Plot

Discover the enchanting tale of Nobody Owens, a boy raised in a graveyard by ghosts. As he navigates the mysteries and dangers of the supernatural world, Nobody learns valuable lessons about life, love, and the power of friendship. This haunting and heartwarming story will captivate readers of all ages.
