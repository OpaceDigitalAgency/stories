## The Invention of Hugo Cabret Plot

In this captivating novel, meet Hugo Cabret, a young orphan living in the walls of a Paris train station. Hugo's talent for fixing clocks and his quest to uncover a secret message left by his late father lead him on a breathtaking adventure. This unique story is told through a combination of words and stunning illustrations.
