## The Midnight Gang Plot

When the clock strikes midnight at the Lord Funt Hospital, a secret gang of children with unusual illnesses embarks on exciting adventures. Join them as they navigate the hospital's corridors and encounter eccentric characters, heartwarming moments, and the magic of friendship. This enchanting tale is packed with humor, imagination, and David Walliams' signature wit.
