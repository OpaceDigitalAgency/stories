## The Mystery of the Whistling Caves Plot

Join Scott and Jack during their summer with their great aunt as they explore the lighthouse, the castle, and the mysterious whistling caves. When priceless treasures are stolen and the caves stop whistling, the friends must solve the mystery and catch the thief before it's too late!
