## The Peppers and the International Magic Guys Plot

Join Monty and Esme Pepper, the mischievous Pepper twins, on a show-stopping adventure. When their Uncle Potty's magic organization, International Magic Guys, faces closure, it's up to the Pepper twins to save the day. With real magic tricks, eccentric characters, and a dash of mayhem, this book will leave you spellbound!
