## The Worst Witch Plot

Meet Mildred Hubble, the worst witch at Miss Cackle's Academy for Witches. Follow her hilarious and often disastrous adventures as she tries to fit in, learn spells, and prove herself to her teachers and classmates. This classic tale of friendship, magic, and self-discovery has been enchanting readers for decades.
