## Summary

A delightful poem capturing the essence of autumn with its vibrant colors, falling leaves, and the animals preparing for the season.

## Story

Animals Hibernating

U is for the hedgehogs Under the leaves

T is for the Trees swishing in the autumn wind

U is for the birds Up in the sky

M is for Me playing in the leaves

N is for nuts falling from the trees.
