## Story

Once there was a cat called Milly she was a brave little cat who liked adventures. She had silky black fur that gleamed like the moon and shining blue eyes like sun beams. Now this story begins when Milly was five years old she said to her mum I'm going to see the world said Milly. But Milly said her mother you are only five years old. I know said Milly so she packed a bag and left...
