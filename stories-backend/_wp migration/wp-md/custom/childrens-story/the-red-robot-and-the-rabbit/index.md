## **Author**

**Names:** Ella, Willow, Carlotta, Jodie

**Age:** 10-11

**Location:** Poole, England

## Summary

Join the red robot and the rabbit on a whimsical adventure in a leafy lane where kindness and friendship bloom, and a mischievous rabbit learns the joy of being good for a bowl of rice.

## The Story

The red robot and the rabbit, met down a leafy lane... where the rabbit was being a pain the robot said, "be nice and if you're good I'll give you some rice".
