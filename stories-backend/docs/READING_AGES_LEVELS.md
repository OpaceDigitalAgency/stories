# 📘 UK Reading Age Groups and Lexile Levels

A simplified guide to reading stages in the UK, aligned with Lexile levels for easy understanding.

## 📚 Reading Age Groups and Lexile Alignment

### 👶 Ages 0–4 (Pre-Lexile Development)

```
| Age Group | Typical Activities / Skills                | Reading Stage          | Lexile Equivalent     |
|-----------|---------------------------------------------|------------------------|------------------------|
| 0–12 mo   | Listening to voices, looking at pictures     | Pre-literacy (Sensory) | N/A (too early)       |
| 12–24 mo  | Responding to stories, pointing at objects   | Pre-literacy (Naming)  | N/A                   |
| 2–3 yrs   | Repeating phrases, “reading” from memory     | Pre-literacy (Mimicry) | BR (Beginning Reader) |
| 3–4 yrs   | Identifying letters, understanding sequences | Early Pre-reader       | BR (Beginning Reader) |
```

> **Note:** Lexile levels officially start with measurable reading ability, usually from age 5. "BR" indicates Beginning Reader level (below 0L).

---

## 🔍 Understanding Lexile Measures

- **Lexile Measure:** A number indicating a reader’s ability or the difficulty of a text (e.g., 850L).
- **Comfortable Range:** Best reading growth occurs within 100L below to 50L above a reader's current Lexile score.
- **How It’s Measured:** Assessments like Scholastic Reading Inventory (SRI) and Star Reading provide Lexile scores.

## 🛠️ Tips for Book Selection

- Choose books near your child’s Lexile level for optimal comprehension.
- Look for variety in topics and genres to support vocabulary and critical thinking.
- Regularly assess progress using Lexile-based tools and adjust accordingly.
